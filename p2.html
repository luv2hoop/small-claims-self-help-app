Getting ready to file

Official links

  • Forms page
  • Guide & File
  • Idaho Code Title 5, Chapter 2 (deadlines)

Before filing, it helps to gather:

  • The defendant's name(s), address(es), and phone number(s)
  • The date your claim arose and the amount
  • Any supporting documents

Naming a business defendant correctly matters. If the business is a sole proprietorship, list the owner's name and the business name. The Idaho Secretary of State's office can help you find a business owner's name.

The most common form mistake: leaving something blank. Fill in every part of the form — write “N/A,” “none,” or “unknown” if needed.

Deadlines exist. Different kinds of cases have different filing deadlines (statutes of limitations). If you miss your deadline, you won't be able to file your claim. To know the deadline for your case, ask a lawyer or read the state law.

Get the official forms (fillable, in Word and PDF). You can also use Guide & File, an online tool that prepares your forms.

The Court Assistance Office (CAO) can clerically review your forms before you file — optional but recommended. You're welcome to file without review.
