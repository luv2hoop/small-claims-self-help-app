Small Claims Self-Help Guide

Who can help me?

Reach for each resource when you need it — we are more than happy to help! If you'd like your forms clerically reviewed before filing, contact the Court Assistance Office. If you're ready to file, contact Court Records. If you have general process questions, contact any of the resources below. If you'd like legal advice, consult with an attorney.

Local (Bannock County)

  • Court Assistance Office — 624 E Center St, Room 106, Pocatello — 208-236-7067 — text 208-538-4816
    Scheduled in-person appointments Wednesdays; walk-in Mondays until 4pm and Friday mornings until 12pm; phone/text/email 8am–5pm M–F.
  • Court Records / Small Claims Clerk — Room 108 — 208-236-7000
    8am–4:45pm M–F. Files documents; general process questions.
  • Family Court Scanning Service — Room 106
    Free scanning Tue & Thu partial days (hours vary); during walk-in hours or scheduled appointments.
  • Libraries — Marshall Public Library, Portneuf District Library, ISU Law Library
    Public computers, low-cost printing/scanning, legal resources.
