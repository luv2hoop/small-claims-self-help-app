Pick the kind of deadline, enter the date it starts counting from, and this tool will add up the days for you.

Deadline:

This calculator only counts calendar days from the date you enter. It does not know about court holidays or other rule exceptions, and it is not legal advice. Always confirm your exact deadline with the clerk.

About the deadline for serving the defendant

The deadline to serve the defendant and file proof of service is either 30 or 90 days, depending on the court — Bannock County's exact number hasn't been confirmed for this guide yet. Ask the clerk which applies to your case, then use “Other” above with the number they give you. See Serving the defendant.

About mediation timing

Mediation follows a different kind of rule — it counts specific Fridays, not a flat number of calendar days — so it isn't included in this calculator. See Mediation for how that timing works.
