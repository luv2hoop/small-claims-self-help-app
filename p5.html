The defendant's 21 days

Official links

  • Idaho Code § 1-2303
  • I.R.S.C.A. Rule 4

  • Form CAO SC 5-1

After being served, the defendant has 21 days to file an Answer with the court.

If the defendant answers: the court orders both parties to mediation (see the next step).

If the defendant does not answer: you can ask for a default judgment. In Bannock County:

  1. Start the case.
  2. Serve the defendant.
  3. Wait the full 21 days.
  4. File the Affidavit for Default, of Competence, Non-Military Service, and Amount Due (form CAO SC 5-1).

Even in a default, the judge must be satisfied by the evidence supporting the claim.

Counterclaims are not allowed in small claims. A defendant who has their own claim against you files a separate case.

If you (the plaintiff) don't show up for a hearing, the court may dismiss your case.
