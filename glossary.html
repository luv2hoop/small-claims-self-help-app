Small Claims Self-Help Guide

Court words in plain language

Plaintiff
The person or business who files the lawsuit.
Defendant
The person or business being sued.
Complaint (Claim)
The written form that starts the case and states the money or property owed.
Answer
The defendant's written reply to the claim.
Service / Serve
Giving official copies of court papers to the defendant.
Proof of service
A signed paper showing the defendant received the court documents.
Default judgment
A win for the plaintiff if the defendant fails to show up or answer.
Without prejudice
Dismissal that allows the plaintiff to refile the case later.
Judgment
The final decision by the court about who wins and how much is owed.
Execution / Writ
A court order used to take property or money from the loser to pay the debt.
Trial de novo (“new appeal trial”)
A brand-new trial restarting an appeal from small claims.
Magistrate
The judge who hears the small claims case in an Idaho magistrate court.
Venue
The correct county court location to file the case.
Mediation
A meeting with a neutral helper to try and settle the dispute before seeing a judge.
