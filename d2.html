Filing your Answer

Three ways to complete and file:

  1. Guide & File (online): prepares your forms and lets you e-file. guideandfile.idaho.gov
  2. eFile & Serve: businesses in e-filing counties must e-file. idaho.tylerhost.net/ofsweb
  3. Print and file in person: get the forms or pick them up at the courthouse. Sign and date, make at least two copies (one for you, one for each Plaintiff), and take them to the court where the Claim was filed.

After you answer, the court orders mediation (see the Mediation step — same process for both sides) and, if needed, schedules a trial and mails both parties the notice.

If you don't attend your trial, the judge may grant a default judgment as if you hadn't answered.

To prepare, see Get Ready for Your Small Claims Trial — Defendant.
